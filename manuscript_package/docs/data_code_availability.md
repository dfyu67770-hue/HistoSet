# Availability of Data and Materials

The public and third-party accessible datasets supporting this study are listed in Additional file 2: Table S1. The concept-labeled TissueArray-derived resource reported by Mittmann et al. and deposited in Figshare (doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.27301845; accessed 23 July 2026) was used for explanation-level supervision. Gleason2019 images and masks were accessed from the Grand Challenge platform (https://gleason2019.grand-challenge.org/; accessed 23 July 2026). Harvard OCYCMP images and masks were accessed from Harvard Dataverse (doi:10.7910/DVN/OCYCMP; accessed 23 July 2026). Gleason2019 and Harvard OCYCMP were used for pattern-level supervision and evaluation after label harmonization. Derived machine-readable figure source data are included in Additional file 3.

The prepared HistoSet tensors, model predictions, and figure-generation tables were derived from public source datasets. Source images and masks are not redistributed in the manuscript package. Redistribution of derived image tensors follows the access conditions and licences of the corresponding source datasets, including Grand Challenge restrictions on data sharing where applicable. The analysis scripts required to regenerate the prepared tensors and figures are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.
